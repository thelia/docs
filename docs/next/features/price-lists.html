Price Lists

Added in Thelia 3.4.

A price list is a named set of prices for chosen combinations. Use it for a wholesale tariff, a negotiated price for one customer, or prices that drop with the quantity ordered. A list is assigned to customer groups or to single customers, and the customers who match it pay its prices.

What a list holds​

FieldMeaning
CodeUnique, up to 64 characters: letters, digits, dots, dashes and underscores. Change it with whoever refers to the list by code.
TitleTranslatable.
CurrencyThe currency of the prices of the lines. It cannot change once the list has lines.
PriorityThe list with the smallest number is looked up first.
Starts at, ends atBoth optional. The list applies from the start date and stops at the end date. The start must be before the end.
ActiveA list that is turned off applies to nobody.
ExclusiveSee Exclusive lists.

A list has lines. Each line is for one combination (a product sale element) and holds:

  • a minimum quantity, 1 by default: the line applies from this quantity up;
  • either a price, without tax, in the currency of the list, or a discount in percent of the catalog price (more than 0, 100 at most). Never both.

A list has one line per combination and minimum quantity.

Managing lists in the back office​

Open Prices and assortments > Price lists. The menu needs the admin.pricing right.

  1. Create the list with a code and a title, then set its currency, priority, dates and flags.
  2. Add lines one by one, or import them from a CSV file.
  3. Assign the list under Groups the list is assigned to and Customers. Every customer of a selected group gets the list. A list can be assigned once to the same group or the same customer. Searching customers needs the right to view them.

To check the result, open a product in Catalog. The Effective prices panel shows, for a customer you search or for a visitor, the price of each combination, the list, rule or group that produced it, whether it can be bought, and the quantity tiers. It needs the right to view admin.pricing and admin.customer.

Which list applies​

The lists that apply to a customer are the ones that are active, inside their period, and assigned to the customer or to their group. They are sorted by priority, lowest first. At equal priority, a list assigned to the customer comes before one assigned to their group, then the oldest list comes first. A visitor, a console command and a guest account use the lists of the default group. A guest account has no list of its own.

For a combination and a quantity, the first list that has a line wins. Inside that list, the line with the highest minimum quantity that is not above the quantity asked is used.

Example. A group list with priority 30 holds A at 50 and B at 60. A customer list with priority 10 holds A at 45. A customer of that group who has the customer list pays 45 for A, 60 for B, and the catalog price for any other combination.

A list price is final. No group price, group discount, catalog price rule, reserved sale or customer discount applies on top of it. A line in percent applies to the catalog price, never to the promotional price. A list in another currency than the one browsed prices nothing. A bundle priced by its components ignores price lists.

See How the price is decided for where the lists sit among the other prices.

Exclusive lists​

A customer who has an exclusive list can buy only the combinations that list holds. The other products stay visible with their catalog price, and they cannot be added to the cart. The front API answers 422 on POST /api/front/cart_items.

  • This holds in every currency. The list prices its combinations in its own currency. In another currency, the lists below it and then the catalog price them.
  • A combination that the list holds only for a higher quantity is still held. It is bought at the price below.
  • A list with a higher priority (a smaller number) that does have a line for the combination can still price it and open it. A list ranked after the exclusive one cannot.

Quantity tiers​

The cart prices each line at its quantity, and prices it again when the quantity changes. The front API publishes priceTiers on a sale element, and the Flexy theme shows them as a Price by quantity table on the product page.

The cart​

The cart line keeps the list that priced it (cart_item.price_list_id). Turning off or deleting that list, or signing in a visitor whose customer has a list, prices the cart again before the payment.

Importing and exporting lines​

A CSV file adds or updates the lines of a list. The file is at most 5 MB. The separator is a semicolon or a comma, read from the header line. The columns:

ColumnMeaning
pse_ref or pse_idThe reference or the id of the combination. One of them is required.
min_quantityOptional.
price or discount_percentOne of them is required. A line holds one amount only.

A line is identified by the combination and the minimum quantity. It is updated if it exists and created otherwise. A line in error is not written, and the others are. The report gives the line number of the file and the reason.

In the back office, Import lines from a CSV file shows a preview first: how many lines would be created, how many updated, and the errors with their reason. Nothing is written until you confirm. Export CSV writes the lines in the same columns. The export is logged.

From the command line:

php Thelia thelia:price-list:import <code> <file>             # write the lines
php Thelia thelia:price-list:import <code> <file> --dry-run   # report only

The command exits with a non-zero code when a line is in error or the file is refused. See thelia:price-list:import.

For developers​

The admin API​

All of these need the admin.pricing right. Each has a collection, an item, creation, PUT, PATCH and deletion.

RouteHolds
/api/admin/price_listscode, currency, priority, exclusive, startsAt, endsAt, active, translated i18ns. Filters on code, currency.id, active, exclusive; orders on priority and code.
/api/admin/price_list_itemspriceList, productSaleElements, minQuantity, price (without tax, in the currency of the list), discountPercent. Filters on priceList.id and productSaleElements.id.
/api/admin/price_list_assignmentspriceList, and exactly one of customerGroup or customer.

A line needs a price or a discount, not both, and is unique by list, combination and minimum quantity. An assignment is refused with a 422 when it names both a group and a customer, or neither, or when the list is already assigned to that group or customer. Assignments are never disclosed to the storefront.

On the front API, a sale element publishes priceTiers, purchasable and priceDisplayMode. See Price display.

Services​

  • Thelia\Domain\Pricing\CustomerPrice\CustomerPriceContextInterface::resolve() takes an optional last argument array $quantities (sale element id to quantity). ResolvedPrice has purchasable, priceListId and tiers.
  • Thelia\Domain\Pricing\PriceList\Import\PriceListLineImport has preview() and apply(), as the back office and the command use them. Lines are written in batches of 500, each in its own transaction.

Caches​

The data access cache keys the price paths by signed-in or anonymous visitor while some price is hidden. A customer with a list of their own is not served from it. Writing a list, a line, an assignment or a price display mode flushes it and the shared cache. Anonymous pages are not shared while a list is assigned to the default group.

A shop without a price list, an assortment rule or a hidden price runs no more queries than before.
