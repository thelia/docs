Assortments and Price Display

Added in Thelia 3.4.

Two features decide who sees which part of the catalog, and who sees prices:

  • Assortments open or close categories, brands and products to a customer group or to a single customer.
  • Price display hides prices from visitors, or from everyone, and replaces them with a login invitation or a price request.

Assortments​

An assortment rule is one line: a customer group or a customer, a category, a brand or a product, and a direction.

DirectionEffect
AllowThe target is open. Whatever no allow rule opens is closed.
DenyThe target is closed, whatever an allow rule says.

A category stands for its whole subtree. A rule names exactly one customer group or one customer, and exactly one category, brand or product.

How the rules combine​

A customer is bound by the rules of their group and by their own rules, taken together. A visitor, a guest account and a console command use the rules of the default group.

  • A deny wins over an allow.
  • A customer with no allow rule sees everything that no deny rule closes.
  • Once a customer has an allow rule, everything that no allow rule opens is closed.
  • A product filed in no category is opened only by an allow rule on the product or on its brand.
  • A brand stays listed while it carries a product the customer can see.

Assortments add to the list of categories of a customer group. A product is visible when both open it.

The rules apply wherever the catalog is read: listings, search, the sitemap, the front API (404 on an item), the brand loop and the cart. A closed product cannot be added to the cart. A product that became closed is removed when the cart is loaded again. The back office and the admin API are never narrowed.

In the back office​

Open Prices and assortments > Assortments, then create a rule. It needs the admin.pricing right. Choose who the rule applies to (customer groups, one rule per group, or a customer you search by name, e-mail or reference), what it is about (a category, a brand or a product), and the direction.

On a product page, the Effective prices panel tells you when the product is outside the assortment of a customer you search.

Through the API​

/api/admin/catalog_restrictions has a collection, an item, creation, PUT, PATCH and deletion. A rule has customerGroup or customer, one of category, brand or product, and mode (allow or deny). It can be filtered by mode, customerGroup.id and customer.id. The rules are never disclosed to the storefront.

For developers​

Thelia\Domain\Catalog\Restriction\CatalogRestrictionResolver reads the rules that bind a customer, once per request. Thelia\Domain\Catalog\Product\ProductVisibility takes it as a constructor argument. It is not a tagged ProductVisibilityRuleInterface, so hasModuleRules() still answers for the rules of modules only.

Price display​

The way prices show is a mode:

ModeBehaviour
visibleEvery visitor sees the prices and may buy. This is the default.
logged_inA visitor sees no price and no add-to-cart button, and is invited to sign in. A signed-in customer sees prices as usual.
on_requestNobody sees a price. A Request a price button leads to the contact form.

An unknown value falls back on visible.

Setting the mode​

The shop default is the Display of the prices setting in Configuration > Store (price_display_mode). A product can override it. On the product page, Display for this product offers Like the shop, or one of the three modes.

The contact form opens with the references only (product_ref and pse_ref), never a price.

What a hidden price means​

When a price cannot be shown, it is null wherever the core publishes it: in the front API, in ProductSaleElementsAccessService::psesByProduct(), in the product and product_sale_elements loops, and in the quick order. The sale elements of the front API also carry priceDisplayMode and purchasable.

  • Sorting and filtering by price do not reveal the hidden price.
  • Adding such an element to the cart is refused, even by a direct call.

Shared cache​

A shop that hides its prices from visitors should review its shared cache. Anonymous pages are not shared while a price is hidden. The data access cache keys the price paths by signed-in or anonymous visitor while some price is hidden. Writing a price display mode flushes it and the shared cache.

For developers​

Thelia\Domain\Pricing\PriceVisibility\PriceDisplayMode is the enum behind the modes, with showsPrice() and purchasable(). PriceVisibilityResolver is the one place every reader of a price and every door of the cart ask: it answers from the mode of the product, else the one of the shop. A shop where every price is visible pays no extra query.

Several services take new constructor arguments with no default, after their existing ones. A module that extends or decorates one of them passes the new arguments:

  • Thelia\Api\EventListener\EffectivePriceListener and Thelia\Api\Service\DataAccess\ProductSaleElementsAccessService: PriceVisibilityResolver, then PriceTierPublisher.
  • Thelia\Api\EventListener\EffectivePriceCollectionWarmer and Thelia\Api\Bridge\Propel\Filter\CustomFilters\ProductFilter\ProductPriceOrderFilter: PriceVisibilityResolver.
  • The brand loop Thelia\Core\Template\Loop\Brand: CatalogRestrictionResolver and CurrentCustomerProvider.
