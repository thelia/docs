Accounting Exports

Added in Thelia 3.4.

Two exports give the accountant the sales of a period in a file their software loads, instead of an order export to rework: the sales journal, one row per accounting entry, and the tax summary, one row per month and per tax rate. They sit in the Orders category of the export screen and run from the command line like any export.

The chart of accounts​

Set it in the back office, under Configuration > Accounting, before the first export:

SettingExampleMeaning
Journal code and labelVE, Ventesthe sales journal; VE / Ventes when left empty
Customer account411000debited of every invoice
Shipping account708500credited of the shipping, excluding tax
One row per tax rate20 → 706200, 445720the account of the sales at that rate, and of the tax collected (not needed at 0)
Account of commercial credit notes709000optional: debited of the commercial credit notes; the sales account of their rate when left empty

They are the accounting_* configuration variables, which thelia:config reads and writes; the rates are one variable, accounting_rate_accounts, as 20:706200:445720,5.5:706055:445705,0:706000. The sales journal refuses to run until the chart is complete, and says what is missing: no account is ever made up.

The sales journal​

The invoices of the period, by invoice date: an order placed in January and invoiced in February is in February. An order without invoice reference, or without invoice date, has no entry; the report counts them (see The report).

Each invoice is a piece, balanced to the cent:

  • the customer is debited of the total of the invoice;
  • the products are credited excluding tax, one line per tax rate of the chart; prices and taxes being rounded to the cent, an amount is filed under the rate of the chart that gives its tax within a cent (0.20 of tax on 0.99 makes 20.20%, filed at 20%), and a line taxed twice under the sum of its rates, which the chart needs a row for;
  • an order discount is spread over the rates in proportion of their amount excluding tax;
  • the shipping is credited excluding tax on the shipping account, at the rate or rates of its tax; untaxed shipping needs no rate row;
  • the tax collected is credited by rate, the shipping's included.

The amounts are those of the invoice handed to the customer, under the rounding rule the order was priced with, older orders included: nothing is recomputed. An order priced before the rounding rules did not take its discount off the tax on its invoice, and is booked so. An order in another currency is booked in the shop currency at the rate of the order, with its own amount and currency beside it. An order that cannot be booked, for instance taxed at a rate the chart has no account for, is left out and named in the report.

A discount on an order with several rates​

Thelia takes the tax out of an order discount at the average of the rates of the order lines, not weighted by their amounts. On an order that mixes rates, the invoice then collects a little more tax at one rate and a little less at another than its amounts excluding tax say. The journal books the invoice as it was issued, and the report names each such order with the tax collected and the tax the rate gives: check them before the tax return.

Credit notes​

When the CreditNote module is active, its credit notes are in the sales journal: a credit note is booked once it has a credit note number and an invoiced status (accepted or used), at its date, between the invoices of the same period. Each one is the reverse of an invoice: the customer is credited, the products, the shipping and the tax it gives back are debited by rate. A credit note in another currency is booked at its own exchange rate, or at the rate of its order when it has none. CreditNote 4.4.0 records no rate on its credit notes, so the rate of the order is the one used: the credit note then reverses its invoice to the cent. A credit note with neither a rate nor an order is left out and named in the report.

A commercial credit note (a rebate, a discount, a refund of a difference) is a reduction granted after the invoice: it is debited to the account of commercial credit notes when it is set (709 in the French chart), to the sales account of its rate otherwise. A discount granted on the invoice itself stays on the sales account, net.

A credit note used to pay an order is a settlement, not a sale given back: it is not in the sales journal. The tax summary takes the credit notes off their month.

The journal reads the tables of CreditNote 4.4.0. When the module is active but its tables cannot be read, or when no credit note module is installed, the report says that the credit notes are not in the journal: refunds made without a credit note never are.

Formats​

The columns of the sales journal are the 18 fields of the French accounting entries file (fichier des écritures comptables), in their order:

FieldValue
JournalCode, JournalLibthe sales journal
EcritureNum, PieceRefthe invoice reference
EcritureDate, PieceDate, ValidDatethe invoice date
CompteNum, CompteLibthe account and its label
CompAuxNum, CompAuxLibthe customer reference and name, on the customer line only
EcritureLibFacture <reference> <customer>, Avoir <reference> <customer> for a credit note (in the language of the export)
Debit, Creditthe amount, in the shop currency
EcritureLet, DateLetempty
Montantdevise, Idevisethe amount and currency of an order in another currency

Choose the FEC format to get the file itself: tab separated, the field names on the first line (even for a period without invoice), dates as YYYYMMDD, amounts with a decimal comma, UTF-8. Rename it <SIREN>FEC<YYYYMMDD>.txt before handing it to the tax administration. Choose CSV to read it in a spreadsheet. The labels follow the language chosen for the export. The FEC format is offered to the sales journal only: the other exports, the tax summary included, are refused in that format, and the imports do not read it.

The FEC file is written as the format asks, without the guard the other exports put in front of a value a spreadsheet would read as a formula (a customer name starting with =, for instance). Open it in accounting software, not in a spreadsheet.

The tax summary​

Per month of invoice and per tax rate, the highest first: the amount taxed (products and shipping), the tax and the number of pieces (invoices and credit notes). It is read from the same pieces as the sales journal, so its taxes add up to the tax lines of the journal, to the cent: the figures to check the tax return against.

The report​

The page of the export job shows what the export did, once it is done: the number of pieces and the totals of debit and credit, the number of credit notes among them, the orders and credit notes left out and why, the orders whose discount moved tax between rates, and how many orders have no entry:

  • the orders of the period with an invoice date but no invoice reference;
  • the orders placed in the period with an invoice reference but no invoice date: they belong to no period, book them by hand;
  • the orders placed in the period that are not invoiced.

The report names the first 100 warnings and counts the others in a last line, so that a long period never keeps the job from ending.

On the command line, the report follows the path of the file:

php Thelia export thelia.export.sales_journal thelia.fec --start=2026-02-01 --end=2026-02-28 --locale=fr_FR
php Thelia export thelia.export.tax_summary thelia.csv --start=2026-01-01 --end=2026-03-31

Both exports can be sent as scheduled report mails. The mail opens with their totals: the number of pieces, of which credit notes, and the debit and credit of the journal; the amount taxed, the tax collected and the number of pieces of the tax summary.

Keeping the files, and anonymized customers​

The sales journal of a closed period is a document to keep: export it at the closing and keep the file for as long as the law asks you to keep your accounting records. The shop does not keep it for you: the file of an export is deleted once it is a day old, by the next export or by maintenance:purge, and the export job and its report a week after it is done (see Personal data). The report names orders and credit notes by their reference only.

Anonymizing a customer keeps their orders, amounts, invoice numbers and dates, and the customer reference: a journal exported again after it has the same pieces and the same amounts, but the name of the customer (CompAuxLib) reads anonymous anonymous. The credit notes keep the address the CreditNote module copied, which the anonymization of the core does not reach.
