Product bundles

Added in Thelia 3.4.

A bundle is a product sold as one line that is made of other products. A cycling kit made of a bike, two helmets and an optional lock is one product with one price and one cart line. The stock that moves, the parcel that leaves and the weight that is billed are the ones of its components.

A shop that has no bundle runs exactly as before. The feature only costs something once a product becomes a bundle.

Set up a bundle in the back office​

  1. Open the product, tab General, and tick Product bundle in the Bundle card.
  2. Choose the Bundle price: Fixed price or Sum of the components (see Prices). With the sum, set a Bundle discount and its Discount type, Percent or Amount.
  3. Save. A Composition tab appears next to Combinations.
  4. In Composition, pick a category, a product, then the combination the bundle is made of, and set the Quantity, an optional Minimum and Maximum, and whether the component is Required. Click Add.

The Composition tab starts with a summary: the sum of the components, the bundle price, the discount, the availability, and a warning when the bundle cannot be sold (no component yet, a required component offline or deleted, a component out of stock). Each component can be edited, moved or removed from the list below.

What a component is​

A component is one combination of another product, never the product itself. It has:

FieldMeaning
QuantityThe quantity in one bundle. It is the fixed quantity when no minimum and no maximum are set.
Minimum, MaximumThe bounds a customer may choose within. Both are optional.
RequiredA required component is always part of the bundle. An optional one can be left out when its minimum is 0.
PositionThe display order.

The rules:

  • A bundle keeps one technical combination. Its stock is ignored. A product that already has several combinations cannot become a bundle, and a bundle cannot get a second combination.
  • A bundle holds no bundle. A bundle cannot be added as a component, and a product that is already a component cannot become a bundle.
  • A combination appears once in a bundle.
  • A component keeps its combination. To change it, remove the component and add another one.
  • Deleting a combination that is a component keeps the line, empty. If that component was required, the bundle cannot be sold until the line is removed or replaced. The back office flags it.
  • Cloning a product copies its bundle settings and its components.

Prices​

ModePrice of the bundle
fixedThe price of the bundle's own combination. Promotions, sales and catalog rules apply as for any product.
computedThe sum of the components the customer kept, less a discount in percent or in an amount.

An amount discount is written in the default currency and converted for another currency. The price never goes below 0.

Example, computed mode, 10 % discount:

ComponentQuantityRequiredPrice excl. tax
Bike1yes500
Helmet2yes30
Lock1 (min 0, max 3)no20

The customer picks the lock twice. The sum is 500 + 2 x 30 + 2 x 20 = 600. With 10 % off the price is 540. With an amount discount of 50 it is 550. With the default composition (no lock) the sum is 560, and 504 after 10 %.

The computed price is read from one place, so the product loops, the API, the quick order, the cart and the order all show the same number. Catalog rules and sales do not stack on a computed bundle. They apply to a fixed one.

Availability​

The availability is deduced from the components: for each kept component, the stock divided by its quantity, rounded down, and the smallest result wins. With the example above and the lock chosen twice, min(7 / 1, 5 / 2, 10 / 2) gives 2 bundles. A bundle made only of virtual components is unlimited.

What the customer does​

The customer picks a composition, within the bounds, and adds the bundle to the cart as one line. The cart refuses:

  • a quantity outside the bounds of a component, or a required component missing;
  • a component that is offline, deleted, hidden from the customer, or itself a bundle;
  • a quantity the components cannot supply, when the shop checks stock.

The messages are translated and name the component.

Two bundles with a different composition are two lines. The same composition merges into one.

At the order, the bundle is one priced line, and each component is a line with a price and a tax of 0, linked to its bundle line. The stock of the components is taken, never the stock of the bundle's own combination. Components are shipped, the bundle line is not. A customer returns the bundle line, refunded at its own price, and the components go back in stock in proportion. A component cannot be returned alone.

The bundle is taxed with the tax rule of the bundle product. A bundle of components at different rates is taxed as a whole at the rate of the bundle product: check the consequence for VAT before selling one.

The weight is the one of the bundle's combination when it is above 0, and the sum of the components otherwise.

Developer reference​

API​

Product gains isBundle (read and write on the admin API, read on the front API), bundlePriceMode (fixed or computed), bundleDiscount and bundleDiscountType (percent or amount). A missing field leaves the stored value alone.

Components are the product_bundle_items resource:

OperationRouteAccess
Create, listPOST, GET /api/admin/product_bundle_itemsright product
Read, replace, patch, deleteGET, PUT, PATCH, DELETE /api/admin/product_bundle_items/{id}right product
ReadGET /api/front/product_bundle_items and /{id}public: the sellable components of an online bundle only
POST /api/admin/product_bundle_items
{
  "bundleProduct": "/api/admin/products/42",
  "productSaleElements": "/api/admin/product_sale_elements/77",
  "quantity": 1,
  "minQuantity": 0,
  "maxQuantity": 3,
  "required": false
}

Changing the productSaleElements of an existing item answers 422. So does a second combination posted to /api/admin/product_sale_elements for a bundle.

To add a bundle to a cart, POST /api/front/cart_items takes bundleComposition, a list of {bundleItemId, quantity}. Neither price nor stock is read from the body.

POST /api/front/cart_items
{
  "cart": "/api/front/carts/9",
  "productSaleElements": "/api/front/product_sale_elements/310",
  "quantity": 1,
  "bundleComposition": [
    {"bundleItemId": 12, "quantity": 1},
    {"bundleItemId": 13, "quantity": 2},
    {"bundleItemId": 14, "quantity": 2}
  ]
}

PUT /api/front/cart_items/{id} refuses a composition different from the one of the line. The cart line stores the choice as a canonical JSON in cart_item.bundle_composition, which is what tells two lines of the same bundle apart.

Order lines​

order_product.line_type is bundle on the bundle line and bundle_component on each component, whose parent is the id of the bundle line. Other lines are product, as before. The OrderProduct model gains isBundleLine(), isBundleComponentLine(), isShippableLine() and movesStockOfItsOwn().

A module that reads order lines (an invoice, a parcel, a picking list) should:

  • show the bundle line and indent its components, using parent;
  • read the lines where isShippableLine() is true to build a parcel, never the bundle line;
  • expect a price and a tax of 0 on component lines.

GET /api/front/account/orders/{id} exposes lineType and parent on each order product.

Events​

BundleCompositionService is the only writer of components. The back office reaches it through these events, handled by Thelia\Action\ProductBundleItem:

ConstantEvent class
TheliaEvents::PRODUCT_BUNDLE_ITEM_ADDProductBundleItemEvent
TheliaEvents::PRODUCT_BUNDLE_ITEM_UPDATEProductBundleItemEvent
TheliaEvents::PRODUCT_BUNDLE_ITEM_DELETEProductBundleItemEvent
TheliaEvents::PRODUCT_BUNDLE_ITEM_UPDATE_POSITIONProductBundleItemPositionEvent

A refused change throws BundleDefinitionException, with a translated message. The admin API does not dispatch these events: it calls the service directly.

Templates​

Read the composition of a cart line with attr('cart', 'cart_items'): each bundle line carries bundle_components, plus bundle_orderable and bundle_refusal that say whether the order would accept the line. The cart loop STOCK output follows the components.

Known limits​

  • SQL sorts and filters on price or stock read the bundle's own combination. A bundle is left out of an "in stock" filter and a sort by price does not see the computed price. The price and quantity that are displayed are right.
  • Reordering from a purchase list takes the default composition.
  • The displayed availability ignores a component reserved to a customer group: the addition is refused instead.
  • A computed bundle takes the national price of its components, even in a sale unit with its own prices.
  • A pack line that the catalog no longer holds stays in the cart total until the customer removes it. The cart step and the order refuse it.
